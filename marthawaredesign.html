How It Works

The Process

Week 1

Phase 1 — Kickoff & Design Vision

Kickoff meeting with questionnaire and site measuring. Concept meeting and floor plan meeting to establish direction.

Week 9

Phase 2 — Sourcing & Main Presentation

Showroom visits as needed. Presentation meeting to review samples, product options, and 3D renderings of your designed space.

Week 12

Phase 3 — Re-Sourcing & Documentation

Fine-tuning selections. Creation of purchasing spec sheets, construction documents, and final renderings.

Week 15

Phase 4 — Pre-Construction

Structural engineering and permitting coordination as needed. Contractor referrals. All documents finalized and sent to your contractor.

Varies

Phase 5 — Purchasing & Construction

We procure all fixtures and furnishings, handle ordering and logistics with White Glove inspection. Construction site visits as needed.

Varies

Phase 6 — Final Details & Photography

Installation of soft furnishings and window coverings. Final styling and coordination of professional photography of your completed home.

Investment

Transparent Pricing

Design fees are typically 10–15% of your construction budget. Smaller single-room projects fall toward the lower end; larger, multi-room projects with custom millwork or structural changes fall toward the upper end.

Design Phase & Final Styling

Flat fee billed 50% upfront and 50% at completion. For projects 2 years or longer, a 20% deposit starts the project with the remainder spread across monthly payments.

Procurement & Construction

All items we purchase on your behalf carry a standard 20% markup. Any trade discounts are passed along to you. Construction site visits are billed hourly.

FAQ

Common Questions

I've never worked with a designer before. Is this for me?

Absolutely! Many people feel nervous that the process will be confusing or that their taste isn't "good enough." My job is to guide you, listen to you, and translate your desires into a space you love. There are no dumb questions.

What's your design style?

My specialty is your style. I don't impose trends on your home. Instead, I focus on what you're naturally drawn to and help you refine it into a cohesive, functional, and beautiful look that will feel authentic for years to come.

Do I have to get rid of all my old furniture?

Not at all! I love helping clients integrate their existing furniture, art, and collectibles into a new design. It's about refreshing and renewing your space—often by working with what you already have and strategically adding new pieces.

What does the process look like?

It starts with a free 20-minute call to connect. If we're a good fit, we schedule an in-home consultation, I provide a detailed proposal, and once you approve, we begin with a kickoff meeting and work through the project in clear phases.
